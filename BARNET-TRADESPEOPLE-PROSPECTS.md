---

## ⚠️ Read this first — data quality

- **All names, phone numbers and review counts below are real** and pulled from live directory listings. Nothing was invented. Where a figure couldn't be confirmed it is marked *(unconfirmed)* or the row is flagged **Unverified**.
- Checkatrade, TrustATrader, MyBuilder, Bark and Yell **block automated reading**, so many review counts come from search-result snippets, not a direct read of the profile. **Re-confirm review count + that there's still no website by opening each link in a browser before you call.**
- **"No website found"** means a targeted `"<name>" + trade + area` search returned only directory/social profiles. Strong signal, not absolute proof — a 30-second manual Google before pitching is worth it.
- **Beware name collisions.** Several leads share a name with a different firm elsewhere that *does* have a site (e.g. "Spencers Builders" → a Gloucestershire firm; "J.S Carpentry" → a Sale firm). These are noted and were verified as different businesses.

## Honest note on the "100" target

I found **~73 verified prospects**, not 100. The reason is a genuine finding, not lack of effort: **in this area, the tradespeople with the most reviews overwhelmingly already have their own websites** (dozens were found and excluded — see each section). The pool of "lots of reviews **and** no website" is naturally smaller than 100. Padding it with first-name-only or unverifiable entries would waste your outreach time, so I kept it to real, checkable leads. If you want to reach 100, the best levers are: (a) widen the area (Enfield, Potters Bar, Borehamwood, Harrow), (b) add more trades (HVAC, drainage, fencing, window/glazing, pest control, cleaning, removals), or (c) lower the review threshold. Say the word and I'll run another batch.
