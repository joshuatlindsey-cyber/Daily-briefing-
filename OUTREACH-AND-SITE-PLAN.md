## 2. Outreach scripts

The pitch in one line: **"You've got dozens of 5-star reviews but no website — so when people Google your name they land on a directory next to your competitors. A simple site puts you first and keeps the lead yours."**

### A. Phone / voicemail (for the ones with public numbers — Clifton, Network Trades, A&A and Sons)
> "Hi, is that [name]? My name's [you], I'm local to Barnet. I came across your [Checkatrade/MyBuilder] profile — genuinely impressed, [X] reviews at [rating] is brilliant. Quick reason for the call: you don't seem to have your own website, and I build simple ones for local tradespeople. It means when someone Googles '[trade] in Barnet' or your name, they find *you* directly instead of a directory listing your rivals too. I can show you a free mock-up using your own reviews — no obligation. Worth a five-minute look?"

### B. Text / WhatsApp (short)
> "Hi [name] — saw your [Checkatrade] profile (the [X] 5★ reviews speak for themselves!). I build websites for Barnet tradespeople and noticed you don't have one yet. Happy to put together a free sample using your reviews so you can see it before deciding anything. Interested? — [you], [number]"

### C. Email / contact-form (for the gated ones — NC Lupu, Tilerservice, Arthur Plastering, A&B, J.S Carpentry)
> **Subject: Your [trade] reviews deserve their own website**
>
> Hi [name],
>
> I'm [you], based in Barnet. I found your [MyBuilder/Checkatrade] profile while looking for a local [trade] — [X] reviews at [rating] is seriously impressive.
>
> One thing I noticed: you don't have your own website. That matters because most customers Google a name before they call, and right now that search sends them to a directory that lists your competitors right alongside you. Your own site fixes that — and it's the one place those great reviews work fully in your favour.
>
> I build clean, mobile-friendly one-page sites for local trades. I'd happily mock one up for you **for free**, using your existing reviews, so you can see exactly what it'd look like before spending a penny.
>
> Want me to send the sample over?
>
> [you] · [phone] · [email]

**Tips:** lead with *their* review count (flattery that's true), offer the free mock-up (kills risk), keep it local ("I'm in Barnet"), one clear ask. The free sample is your strongest move — it's why pulling their real reviews matters.

## 3. Reusable 1-page website outline

Same skeleton for every trade — swap the trade, area and reviews. Mobile-first (most trade searches are on phones).

1. **Sticky header** — Business name + trade · **tap-to-call phone button** (the #1 conversion element for trades) · "Get a free quote".
2. **Hero** — `[Trade] in [Area] you can trust` + one line (`NICEIC-registered · 15+ years · [X] five-star reviews`) + two buttons: **Call now** / **Request a quote**. Background: a real photo of their work.
3. **Trust bar** — review-platform logos + aggregate: ⭐ *"[rating] from [X] verified reviews on [Checkatrade/MyBuilder]"* + accreditations (Gas Safe / NICEIC / NFRC / DBS-checked).
4. **Services** — 4–8 icon tiles of what they do (e.g. rewires, fuse boards, EV chargers…).
5. **Reviews** — 3–6 real testimonials lifted from their profile + a "see all [X] reviews" link to the directory.
6. **Why choose us** — area covered, years' experience, tidy/clean, free quotes, guarantee.
7. **Gallery** — before/after photos of recent jobs.
8. **Service area** — list of postcodes/areas + a small map ("Barnet, Finchley, Whetstone, Edgware…") — helps local Google ranking.
9. **Contact / quote form** — name, phone, job description, postcode + the phone number again + WhatsApp link.
10. **Footer** — phone, email, hours, accreditations, company reg if Ltd.

**Add for findability (cheap, high-impact):** a **Google Business Profile** (free, often more valuable than the site itself for a local trade) and a domain like `[name][trade].co.uk`.
