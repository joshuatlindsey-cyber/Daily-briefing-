    Recent true-ICP discovery calls (the defence PM, the ops lead, the therapist, the construction owner) became paying clients. When the right person is on the call, the framework works. Protect what feeds it.

    Silence is your biggest loss reason.

    One warm, replied, ready-to-book prospect went cold and closed-lost after 12+ days without a reply. A warm collaboration link sat unbooked for four days. Nothing in the call needs fixing there. The follow-up does.

    Sales slots leak to non-buyers.

    A July discovery slot went to a peer swapping advice. Pleasant, useful, never a sale. The qualification gate exists so the calendar only fills with people who can say yes.

    Short calls convert nothing.

    Small promises are conversion events.

    A subscriber who opened seven of seven emails waited days for a recording link you said you would send. Deliver the artefact the same day. Reliability is the pitch for a sceptical, burned-before audience.

    The frame

    Five stages, forty minutes, decision on the call.

    StageTimeJob
    Situation10 minTheir world. Role, years in, the methodology in their head, who depends on them.
    Problem depth10 minShow you understand it better than they do. Ask the three questions. Get a number on the cost of staying.
    Bridge10 minHand their own words back. Name the specific gap. Present 80 Day Exit as the fix for that gap, with one matched client story.
    Ask5 minState the price plainly, once, against the cost of staying. Then stop talking.
    Objections5 minAnswer directly. Decision, deposit, or a dated next step before anyone hangs up.

    Objection library

    The six that actually come up, from your own transcripts. Say it your way, but keep the shape.

    "I've bought courses before and they didn't work."

    This is the meta-question in nearly every one of your discovery calls. Do not defend courses. Agree with them.

    "Good. You should be sceptical. Those courses failed because they were generic and nobody was accountable to you. This is one-to-one, built on your material, and I am on the hook for it. What specifically did the last one not do?"

    Their answer tells you exactly what to promise. Promise only that.

    "I can't afford it right now."

    If you quantified the cost of staying in stage two, this objection is already half-answered. Point back at their own number.

    "You told me staying put is costing you [their number, their words]. £999 against that is not the risky option. And if the timing is genuinely wrong, £100 holds the founder's rate for 90 days. Fully refundable."

    If you never got a number, that is a scorecard zero, not a price problem.

    "I need to talk to my partner."

    For parents this is real, not a dodge. Respect it and make it concrete.

    "Completely right, you should. What is the first thing they will ask? Let's answer it now so you are not selling this alone at the kitchen table. And let's put 20 minutes in the diary for Thursday so it does not drift."

    The dated next step is the close. Never end on "let me know".

    "I don't have the time. I'm done by 8pm."

    Do not sell effort. Your clients are depleted, not lazy. The 4am starts, the two kids, the no-margin weeks are the norm.

    "That is exactly who this is built for. Thirty minutes a day, in the margins, with the system doing the remembering. It replaces scrolling, not sleep. If you had five spare hours a week you would not need me."
    "I'm not sure what I have is worth paying for."

    The dominant fear across every client you have signed. It is an authority problem wearing a competence mask.

    "You are good at this. Ten years of doing the work proves that. What you do not have is proof someone will pay, and confidence follows proof, not the other way round. The whole point of the 80 days is getting that first paying client while the salary is still coming in."
    "My employer can't find out."

    A hard requirement for your ICP, and one of your strongest cards. Say it plainly.

    "That is a design constraint, not a problem. It is why this runs on Threads. Nobody from your office is on Threads, there is no video of your face required, and we build it quiet until you choose otherwise. One client took his discovery call from his car so nobody would hear."
    "Can you send me a proposal to look over?"

    No. Proposals kill your close rate and you already decided this. The ask hides a real objection. Find it.

    "I don't do proposals. Everything you need to decide is in this conversation, and a PDF will not answer anything I cannot answer right now. What is actually unresolved for you?"

    Then handle the real thing, or take the deposit, or set the dated call with the partner. Those are the only three exits.
